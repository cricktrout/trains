Full scale 5 ton H. K. Porter Compressed Air Locomotive S/N 3087 fore sale. The locomotive was built in November, 1904 for the Homestake Mining Co. of Lead SD.

6X10 cylinders and 23 inch drivers. This can be converted to steam operation. or remain as a compressed air locomotive. The locomotives gauge is 18" but as with most small H.K. Porter locomotives can be converted to 24" to standard gauge with new axels and frame cross members. The locomotives is in good overall condition and comes with all parts to operate as an air Locomotive. The air tank, front and rear buffers have been removed awaiting conversion to steam. The locomotive is located in Jacksonville Oregon, 30 miles North of the California- Oregon border on Interstate 5.Asking price is $6500. Will accept Paypal. Sold

Notes: This interesting 2-8-0, Finnish State Railways #418, was built in 1904 and came from Finland in 1960 as a gift to the city of Portland. It was displayed at Oaks Park in Portland with SP 4449, SP&S 700, and UP 3203 for a number of years before going to Junction City. Presently it is under cover and appears to be in good condition.

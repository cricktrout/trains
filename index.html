Glossary
Term Source Summary
F.M. Whyte: Wikipedia A classification notation system created by Frederick Methvan Whyte to classify steam locomotives by wheel arrangement.
Gauge: Wikipedia Track gauge or rail gauge is the distance between the inner sides of the heads of the two load bearing rails that make up a single railway line.
Tractive Effort: Wikipedia A railway engineering term that describes the pulling or pushing capability of a locomotive.
